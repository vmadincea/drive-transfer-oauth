Bible Drive Transfers

A private desktop integration used by its owner to back up, restore, and manage Bible research data, generated videos, and private application snapshots in Google Drive.

How it works

The integration uses rclone on the owner’s computer. Authorized workflows transfer files between that computer and existing Google Drive folders, verify transferred files, and manage owner-approved backup folders.

The OAuth client supports AI-assisted operation under the owner’s direction. Read-only connections use Google Drive read-only access; upload and management connections use Google Drive access.

This is an owner-operated integration. It provides no public signup or customer OAuth login.
