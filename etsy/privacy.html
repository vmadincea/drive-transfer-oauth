Privacy policy

Etsy Drive Transfers is a private desktop integration operated by its owner for Google Drive backups, restoration, file verification, and digital-art delivery workflows.

Google data accessed

Depending on the connection’s authorized scope, rclone can access Google Drive file and folder metadata and file contents, and can upload or manage files and folders. Metadata may include names, identifiers, sizes, timestamps, hashes, and sharing permissions. Read-only connections request drive.readonly; upload and management connections request drive.

Purpose and processing

Data is used to carry out transfers and related verification and management actions authorized by the owner. Files are transferred between the owner’s computer and Google Drive. Delivery files may be shared with intended recipients through owner-approved Google Drive sharing settings.

When the owner uses an AI assistant to operate the integration, relevant file metadata or selected contents may be processed by that assistant’s provider under the owner’s direction. The public website hosting this policy has no OAuth login or access to the owner’s Drive credentials or files.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are kept in the local rclone configuration. Transfer records, selected files, and backups may remain on the owner’s computer or in Google Drive according to the owner’s workflows. The owner manages their retention and deletion.

Access and revocation

The owner can revoke this integration’s access in their Google Account permissions and remove local OAuth credentials. Revoking access does not automatically delete files already stored on the computer or in Drive.

Google API use

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
